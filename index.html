The problem

Your ads work. Your WhatsApp can't keep up.

Click-to-WhatsApp ads bring in a flood of chats — mostly at night, mostly the same ten questions, in three different languages. Every slow reply is a sale that walks away.

Leads go cold

A customer asks "price?" at 11 pm. By the time you answer in the morning, they've bought something else.

Same questions, all day

Price, sizes, COD, delivery time, "is it original?" — typed out by hand, hundreds of times a week.

Important chats get buried

A complaint or a bulk order hides between a hundred "hi"s. Generic chatbots just make customers type "human".

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. Jawaab works on the official WhatsApp Business API, connected through a provider such as MSG91. If you're not on the API yet, we'll walk you through getting set up with your business number.

Will the AI make things up?

It answers only from the business info you write, and it's told never to invent prices, policies, stock or delivery dates. When it isn't sure, or the topic is sensitive, it flags the chat for you. Every decision is logged with its reason, and you can test it on any chat before going live.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Hindi (Devanagari) and Hinglish (Hindi typed in Roman letters), and it replies in the same language as the customer's latest message. Other languages can be added.

Can I keep replying myself?

Of course. Jawaab is first a team inbox. When you reply in a chat, the AI steps back for that conversation. You can also pause the AI for any single chat, or keep it in Suggest mode so nothing goes out without your tap.

What kind of businesses is it for?

Any business where customers ask questions on WhatsApp before they buy: online brands, beauty and fashion, food and bakeries, coaching and education, clinics and wellness, real estate, travel and local services. It works especially well if you run Instagram, Facebook or Click-to-WhatsApp ads.

How much does it cost?

We're in early access and onboarding a small number of brands personally. Pricing will be simple and based on the size of your inbox. AI replies themselves cost only paise each. Write to us and we'll share details for your volume.

Is my customer data safe?

Your chats live in your own workspace, are visible only to your team, and can be exported or deleted at any time.
